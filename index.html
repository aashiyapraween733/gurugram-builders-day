What happens on the day

It isn't a hackathon and nobody wins prizes. It's a relaxed build day where you pair up, get unstuck, and demo whatever you have by the evening.

01

Build

Five hours of focused making. Work solo or join a table with others working on the same stack.

02

Learn

Three short talks on shipping your first web app, using Git and GitHub well, and landing a first internship.

03

Demo

Two-minute demos at 4:30 PM. Unfinished is fine. Showing your work is the point.

04

Connect

Chai, samosas and mentors from local startups who are there to answer questions, not pitch.

FAQ

Quick answers before you sign up.

Is it really free?

Yes. Food and chai are covered, and you don't pay anything to register or attend.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I come?

Please do. The whole day is designed so you can build your first website, and mentors are there to help.

What should I bring?

A laptop, a charger, and a college or government photo ID for check-in.

Do I need a team?

No. Most people come alone and find a table at kickoff.
